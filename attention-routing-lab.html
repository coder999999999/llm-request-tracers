Prompt token-by-token lookback

The chart excludes key position 0 (the first-token attention sink). Where non-sink mass is zero, lookback is undefined and left blank. Values are descriptive statistics, not explanations.

Definitions. Lookback distance = attention-weighted mean (query position − key position) / query position, after excluding key 0, averaged over query positions 2…N−1 where non-sink mass exists. Reply→prompt share is the recorded share of decode-row attention to the original prompt, including sink. First-token shift is decode first-token share minus prompt first-token share. The prompt and reply data come from replayed quantized-model runs; decoding is teacher-forced and not necessarily the original sampling path. First-token shift is a cross-phase descriptive comparison, not a causal score.
